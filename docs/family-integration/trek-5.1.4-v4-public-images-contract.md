No localhost alias, other port, ordinary build or hosted source qualifies. Older image-cache sources retain their exact policy. The general preparation allowlist, private-file admission and whole-readiness policy stay unchanged.

A readonly media-only context selects cover IDs and ready thumb/hero descriptors directly from the actual format. Explicit image preparation uses the existing bounded cache, WebP stream/hash/dimension checks and generation/actor/source/Trip/queue/import fences. Saved display resolves only the exact manifest descriptor to a temporary Blob lease, otherwise a placeholder. Public byte receipts do not grant access to private metadata/documents or imply a fully prepared Trip. Public sharing and private-file acquisition remain refused for v4.

Required proof: semantic real-module old/v4 admission, actual response/cache validation with synthetic bytes, native projection/cold reopening, negative source/format/authority/expiry/generation tests, actual SDK/AAL2 and final-artifact desktop/phone offline/PWA observations. No release or activation claim until its corresponding proof exists. No hosted database/settings/billing, migration, dependency, production/preview/push/publication, original backup/media/ETL or image-transformation operations.
